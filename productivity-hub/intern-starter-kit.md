# Solar Vendor Intern Starter Kit

**Welcome to the Solar Sales Manager Team!**
This guide will help you get started with the Solar Lead Management app — the tool you'll use daily to call vendors, track your progress, and manage your leads.

---

## 1. Logging In

1. Open the app URL in your browser (works on phone and desktop — no app download needed)
2. Go to the **Tools** section
3. Enter your **username** and **password** (given to you by your admin)
4. You'll land on the **Tools Dashboard**

> **Tip**: Bookmark the page for quick access. You can also "Add to Home Screen" on your phone to use it like an app.

---

## 2. Navigating to Solar Leads

From the Tools Dashboard:
1. Click the **"Solar Vendor"** card (yellow icon)
2. You'll see two tabs at the top: **Leads** and **Clients**
3. The **Leads** tab is where you'll spend most of your time

---

## 3. Understanding the Lead Pipeline

Every solar vendor lead goes through these stages:

| Status | What It Means | What You Do |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| **Not Called** | Lead is in the system, no attempt made yet | This is the default for all new leads. Start calling! |
| **No Answer** | You called but the vendor didn't pick up | Try again next day. Stop at **3 attempts** for No Answer leads. |
| **Not Interested** | Vendor picked up but declined | Note the reason in Call Notes. Move on. |
| **Interested** | Vendor showed interest, wants to know more | Schedule a follow-up call or demo. **Set a follow-up date!** |
| **Demo Scheduled** | A product demo or detailed call is booked | Add the date/time in Follow-Up Date. Prepare for the demo. |
| **Converted** | Vendor has signed up / started using Solar Manager | Congratulations! Your admin will convert this lead. |
| **Disqualified** | Wrong number, closed business, duplicate, etc. | Note the reason. Skip in future. |

## 4. Your Daily Workflow

### Step 1: Open Your Leads
- Go to **Solar Vendor > Leads** tab
- The filter at the top defaults to **"My Leads"** — these are the leads assigned to you
- You can search by name, phone, district, or state

### Step 2: Start Calling
For each lead marked **"Not Called"** or with a **follow-up due today**:

1. **Look at the lead card** — it shows:
   - Company name and phone number
   - District/State and number of installations
   - Current status and attempt count
   - Any previous call notes

2. **Click the phone number** to copy it, then make the call

3. **After the call, click "Log Call"** (the blue button on the card)

### Step 3: Log Your Call
The **Log Call** popup asks you three things:

| Field | What to Enter |
|-------|--------------|
| **Call Outcome** | Pick what happened: No Answer, Not Interested, Interested, Demo Scheduled, etc. |
| **Call Notes** | Write 2–3 sentences: What did they say? Are they interested? Any concerns? |
| **Follow-Up Date** | (Only for Interested/Demo Scheduled) When should you call back? |

Then click **"Save Call Log"**.

> **Important**: The app automatically tracks:
> - Today's date as the call date
> - Your attempt count (increments by 1 each time)
> - All your call history is visible to your manager

### Step 4: Send WhatsApp Messages
Each lead card has two WhatsApp buttons:
- **"First WA"** — Send the initial introduction message (in Marathi)
- **"Follow-up WA"** — Send a follow-up reminder after a few days

Click these to open WhatsApp Web with a pre-written message. Just hit send!

### Step 5: Schedule Follow-Ups
For leads marked **Interested** or **Demo Scheduled**:
- Click the **"Follow-up"** button (purple) on the card
- Pick a date and time
- Add notes about what to discuss
- The follow-up will appear on your dashboard

## 6. Using Filters

### Intern Filter (top of Leads page)
- **My Leads** (default) — Shows only leads assigned to you
- You can clear this to see all leads if needed

### Status Filter Pills
Below the search bar, you'll see status buttons:
- Click **"Not Called"** to see only leads you haven't called yet
- Click **"Interested"** to see your warm leads
- Click **"All"** to see everything
- Each pill shows the count of leads in that status

### Search
Type in the search box to find leads by:
- Company name
- Phone number
- District or state name

---

## 7. Importing New Leads from Vendor Database

1. Click the green **"Vendor Prospects"** button at the top
2. A panel opens showing the vendor database
3. Use filters to find vendors by state, district, or installation count
4. Click **"Import as Lead"** on any vendor to add them to your leads list
5. The lead will automatically get:
   - District, state, and installation count filled in
   - Status set to "Not Called"
   - Assigned to you

## 8. Key Rules to Follow

### Calling Rules
- **Maximum 3 attempts** for "No Answer" leads — after 3 tries with no answer, stop calling
- **Always log your call** immediately after — don't batch them at the end of the day
- **Write useful call notes** — "Called, no answer" is not enough. Write what you heard on the voicemail, what time you called, etc.

### Status Rules
- **Not Called → No Answer**: Vendor didn't pick up. Try again tomorrow.
- **Not Called → Not Interested**: Vendor picked up, said no. Log the reason.
- **Not Called → Interested**: Great! Set a follow-up date immediately.
- **Interested → Demo Scheduled**: You've booked a demo time. Add it as a follow-up.
- **Never skip statuses** — don't mark "Interested" without actually talking to them.
- **Disqualified** is for: wrong numbers, businesses that shut down, duplicates.

### Follow-Up Rules
- If a lead is **Interested** or **Demo Scheduled**, you MUST set a **Follow-Up Date**
- Check your follow-ups every morning
- Don't let follow-ups go overdue — the app tracks this

## 9. What Your Manager Sees

Your admin/manager has a **Manager View** that shows:
- **How many calls you made each day**
- **Which leads you called**
- **What status changes you made**
- **Your call notes for each lead**
- **Your follow-up schedule**

This is used for daily/weekly check-in meetings. Keep your data clean and up-to-date!

## 11. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Can't see any leads | Check your filter — make sure "My Leads" is selected, or ask your admin if leads are assigned to you |
| WhatsApp button not working | Make sure the phone number is correct. The number needs to be a valid Indian mobile number |
| Page not loading | Refresh the page. If it still doesn't work, clear your browser cache or try incognito mode |
| Wrong status on a lead | Use the status dropdown on the card to change it. Your change is saved immediately |
| Need more leads to call | Click "Vendor Prospects" to import more vendors from the database |

## 12. Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Lead** | A potential customer (solar vendor) you're contacting |
| **Vendor Prospect** | A vendor from our database that hasn't been imported as a lead yet |
| **Status** | The current stage of a lead in the sales pipeline |
| **Attempt Count** | How many times you've tried calling this vendor |
| **Call Notes** | Your written summary of what happened on the call |
| **Follow-Up Date** | The date you plan to call back |
| **Converted** | A lead that signed up and became a paying customer |
| **Disqualified** | A lead that can't be contacted or isn't relevant |
